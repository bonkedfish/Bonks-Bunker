# Bonk's Bunker alpha3.3: Five Years Gone

Kearney looks like it's been abandoned for five years now, and disc golf got real.

## 🏚️ Realistic houses
- **Every house has real 3D detail:** window frames and sills, a front door facing the street with steps, porches with posts and a roof, garage doors, gutters and chimneys. Flat-roofed buildings get a low wall around the roof.
- **Abandoned, and not all the same way.** Most are grimy, with damp creeping up from the ground, rain streaks under the windows and moss on some. About 1 in 7 is **boarded up**, a few have **burned** (black soot, black roof, charred windows) and some have a **caved-in roof** with broken rafters showing.
- Broken windows everywhere, and boards nailed over some of them.

## 🛣️ Roads and ground
- **Five years of neglect:** cracked asphalt, potholes, patches, weeds pushing through, faded lane lines and dirt washed onto the road edges.
- **Real ground:** dry grass mixed with dirt and greener patches, and **3D grass** that sways around you (quality setting: none on Low).

## 🚗 Abandoned cars
- **About 6,000 realistic wrecked cars** across the map: sedans, pickups, rusty trucks, an old Cadillac, crashed cars and a bus lying on its side.
- **Traffic jams** frozen on the highways and main roads, **half-full parking lots**, and cars left in driveways, some on flat tyres.
- Car trunks can be searched for loot.

## 🥏 Disc golf
- **Real baskets** with link chains, real tee pads and signs, at both Centennial and Cottonmill, weathered like everything else.
- **Five throw styles** (press F): backhand, forehand, putt, tomahawk (it flips over) and roller (it lands on its edge and rolls).
- **Your survivor actually throws:** reach-back, pull-through, release and follow-through, for every style.
- **John Pork sells 20 new discs for bottle caps** (Shop > "Discs. Real ones."): 10 discs, each in **base** or **premium** plastic, from 50 to 140 caps.
  - **Base plastic slips** a little on release and **beats in fast**. A beaten-in disc turns over more and fades less. **Premium** grips and holds its line.
  - How beaten in each disc is gets saved, and shows on the HUD during a round.
- Press **3** to step through the discs you own, longest-flying first. The course box still gives you the three starter discs.

## Known issues
- In first person you don't see your own throw, only the disc after it leaves your hand. Other players see the full swing.
- The disc HUD covers NPC subtitles while you have a disc out.

## Install
**Windows:** unzip and run `BonksBunker.exe`.

**Mac:** unzip, then right-click `BonksBunker.app` > Open the first time (the app isn't notarized). If macOS says it's damaged, run `xattr -cr ` followed by the app's path in Terminal.

**Split downloads:** download every `.001`, `.002` ... part into one folder and open the `.001` with 7-Zip (Windows) or The Unarchiver (Mac). Or join them first. Windows: `copy /b file.zip.001+file.zip.002 file.zip`, Mac: `cat file.zip.0* > file.zip`.

Saves from alpha3.2 carry over.
