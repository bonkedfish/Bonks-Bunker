# Bonk's Bunker alpha3.1: Nights, Strangers & Weird Nebraska

Kearney got a sky, a clock, and a lot of people you did not ask to meet.

## 🌙 Day and night
- **A real day/night cycle.** One full day runs **48 minutes**: sunrise at 06:30, sunset at 19:30.
- Night is **moonlit, not blind.** The moon crosses the sky, casts its own soft shadows, and the sky goes deep blue with **stars**. You can still fight — you just can't see as far.
- Sunrise and sunset colour the whole town.
- **Twice as many zombies after dark**, and far more of them are the nasty ones: Runners, Riot Cops, Spitters and Bloaters all get commoner at night. Shamblers get rarer. Going out at 2 AM is a decision.
- **Sleep it off.** Use either bunk in the bunker at night and **the whole server skips to 07:00** — everyone wakes at full health, fed and watered. You can't sleep through the daylight; the bunk will tell you so.

## 🧟 Four new kinds of zombie
- **Runner** — hears you from much further off and sprints at the noise.
- **Bloater** — bursts when it swings or dies. 50 damage up close, and it hurts raiders and other zombies too.
- **Riot Cop** — helmet, face shield and vest. Body shots do a quarter damage; the head and melee go straight through.
- **Spitter** — ranged, caustic, and content to stay back.
- Which ones you meet depends on **how far you are from the bunker.** Shamblers only, close to home; everything else the further out you go.
- Themed drops: riot armor and ammo off Riot Cops, Caustic Bile off Bloaters and Spitters — which feeds two new recipes, **Pack Shotgun Shells** and **Load 5.56 Rounds**.
- Up to **10 zombies per cell** (20 at night), out of 48 awake anywhere at once.

## ⛳ Safe courses
- **No hostiles on the golf or disc golf courses, ever.** Centennial, Cottonmill, Kearney Country Club and the Drop Zone range are clear, with a 60 m buffer around them. Nothing spawns there, nothing hunts you there, and anything that wanders in walks back out.

## 📖 Act 1 ends, Act 2 begins
- **Collection Day.** The Suits' collection truck has a route and a schedule. Ambush it at the Cash-Wa dock, fight the escort and the Handler, take the manifest, and bring it to Gus. That closes Act 1.
- **South of the Tracks.** Act 2 opens at a grain elevator on the real industrial lot by the Union Pacific spurs east of downtown — yard crew, a logbook, and a hatch in the floor you are not going to like.
- **The man in the suit.** Every ten minutes or so, one player — and only that player — sees someone in a black suit standing 60 to 120 metres off, watching. Walk toward him and he is not there. He does not fight. He does not speak. Nobody else sees him.

## 🎲 Random encounters
Twenty-five of them, spread across the whole map. They open on real roads, out of your sight, never in front of you.

**On the road:** a stranded driver with a dead alternator, a travelling salesman with a questionable inventory, a roadblock with a toll you can pay, bluff or shoot your way past, an abandoned truck that is loot or an ambush, a stranger at a campfire with rumours to trade.

**People:** Fast Eddie the pool champion, a beggar who may or may not be honest, a bounty target in a very ordinary place, a tourist who thinks this is temporary, a drunk who wants to arm wrestle, a gambler, a collector, a con artist selling maps to nowhere, a grave digger who will not say who the grave is for, and a wanderer you will meet again.

**Rare:** a mutated jackrabbit the size of a car, a helicopter crash still smoking, a door standing alone in a field, a skeleton in a lawn chair with a cooler and a radio still playing, and a transmitter counting numbers.

## 🤪 Five people you will not forget
- **Greg** — looking for something. Cannot describe it, name it, or say what it does.
- **The Safety Inspector** — writing up the apocalypse for code violations.
- **The Bargain King** — the worst prices in Nebraska, loudly.
- **The Sheriff of Nothing** — the law in a town with no people.
- **The Chess Master** — will beat you, then accuse you of cheating. Also accuses you of cheating when he loses.

## 🚽 Eighteen things that should not be there
Hand-placed around town, not spawned in front of you. Among them: a skeleton on a toilet still holding an empty roll, an intersection with a king and a cone throne, a man still clocked in at his desk five years later, a shed with three hundred bottles and one key, a skeleton on a treadmill that is somehow still warm, a shop where the mannequins are never quite where you left them, an elevator that works and goes nowhere, a computer with 61,402 games of solitaire played and 3 won, a bathtub full of bottle caps, a skeleton holding a winning ticket drawn the day before, a room where every piece of furniture faces the wall, a sign reading **DEFINITELY NOT A TRAP**, and a vending machine that will take forty caps and think about it for eleven seconds.

## 🏆 Collectibles
- **Fifteen things to hoard**, scattered on 105 spots across Kearney: traffic cones, golf balls, golf tees, playing cards, plungers, garden gnomes, bowling pins, rubber ducks, flip phones, VHS tapes, trophies, model cars, license plates, mannequin heads and lunchboxes.
- They go in your inventory, and **a rack in the bunker fills in as you find them**.

## 👁️ Things that only happen once in a while
Rare, quiet and deliberately unprovable. Someone standing outside the bunker at 3 AM. A mannequin indoors that was not there before. A golf ball rolling to a stop at your feet with nobody around. The radio playing something it should not. A knock at the door with nobody behind it. Lights on in a house you cleared. A car idling where no car was.

Only one player ever sees any of them. Nothing attacks you. Nobody can back you up.

## 🌳 The world itself
- **Real trees:** elm, ash, maple, cottonwood, honey locust, willow, spruce, pine and cedar, with bushes, tall grass, wildflowers and reeds along the shorelines.
- **Weather:** clear, rain and thunderstorms with real lightning, generated rain and thunder. Storms make zombies hear further and push disc golf discs around.
- **A sky with drifting clouds**, which now darkens with the weather and the hour.
- **Golf course dressing:** tee markers, benches, ball washers, 150-yard posts, OB stakes, flags and cups, and shot tracers.

## 🔧 For testers
**F6** opens an encounter debug panel: every encounter with its rarity, real chance and why it is blocked, the open sites, cooldowns, collectible totals and the rare events. TAB switches lists, W/S picks, ENTER force-triggers, C clears cooldowns.

## Install
**Windows:** unzip and run `BonksBunker.exe`.

**Mac:** unzip, then right-click `BonksBunker.app` > Open the first time (the app isn't notarized). If macOS says it's damaged, run `xattr -cr ` followed by the app's path in Terminal.

**Split downloads:** download every `.001`, `.002` ... part into one folder and open the `.001` with 7-Zip (Windows) or The Unarchiver (Mac). Or join them first. Windows: `copy /b file.zip.001+file.zip.002 file.zip`, Mac: `cat file.zip.0* > file.zip`.

Saves from alpha3.0 carry over.
