# Bonk's Bunker alpha3.4: Lock and Load

Zombies are tougher, guns feel like guns, and the motorcycles ride like real bikes.

## 🧟 Tougher zombies, harder-hitting guns
- **Every zombie type has 2.5x the health.** Bosses, raiders and the Suits are unchanged.
- **The pistol is the same, and every other gun now hits harder per shot:**

| Gun | Damage per shot |
|---|---|
| Pistol | 25 |
| SMG | 27 |
| AR | 38 |
| .44 Revolver | 70 |
| Hunting rifle | 110 |
| Shotgun | 16 x 8 pellets |
| Long rifle | 200 |

## 🔫 Guns feel real
- **Recoil:** each gun kicks the view up in its own small pattern, which settles once you stop firing.
- **Spread** grows the longer you hold the trigger and tightens again when you stop.
- **Aim down sights** with right mouse on every gun. It zooms in a little and halves the spread.
- **Muzzle flash** and **brass casings** that fly out and bounce on the floor (red shells for the shotgun).
- **New sounds:** every gun has its own shot sound, and each reload has its own sounds: mag out and in, the slide, shells going in, the revolver opening and closing. The shotgun pumps after each shot, and the rifles work the bolt.
- **A reload bar** under your ammo count.

## ✋ Hands on the guns
- Your survivor holds every gun with **both hands**, in first person and as seen by other players.
- **Draw and holster** when you switch weapons, a **reload** for each gun (magazine swap, revolver loading, shotgun shells one at a time, bolt-action), a **kick** on every shot, and a proper **swing** for the bat, axe and knife.

## 🏍️ Motorcycles ride like bikes
- **Suspension:** the front dives when you brake, the back squats on the gas, and landings bottom it out.
- **Skids:** brake while turning at speed and the back end slides out. Give it gas leaned over in 1st or 2nd gear to power-slide. Sliding helps you turn and never throws you off.
- **Your rider** leans into turns harder than the bike, shifts back on the gas and forward on the brakes, with **hands on the bars and feet on the pegs**.
- **Engine sound** is louder on the gas and deeper in higher gears, and there's a **tyre screech** when you skid.
- **Better bikes:** foot pegs, brake discs, chain, fork, shock spring, radiator, mirrors, and spoked or cast wheels.
- All of it stays in sync in co-op.

## Known issues
- The survivors' guns are cartoon-big in their hands.
- Aiming zooms in and moves the gun toward the middle, but you don't look down the sights.
- On the dirt bike, your rider sits forward near the tank.

## Install
**Windows:** unzip and run `BonksBunker.exe`.

**Mac:** unzip, then right-click `BonksBunker.app` > Open the first time (the app isn't notarized). If macOS says it's damaged, run `xattr -cr ` followed by the app's path in Terminal.

**Split downloads:** download every `.001`, `.002` ... part into one folder and open the `.001` with 7-Zip (Windows) or The Unarchiver (Mac). Or join them first. Windows: `copy /b file.zip.001+file.zip.002 file.zip`, Mac: `cat file.zip.0* > file.zip`.

Saves from alpha3.3 carry over.
