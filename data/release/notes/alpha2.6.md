# Bonk's Bunker alpha2.6: Handling & Comfort

Tweaks to the alpha2.5 features.

## 🏍️ Bikes turn sharper
- The turn radius is about **half** what it was. On full lean at 56 km/h a dirt bike now comes around in roughly 12 m instead of 24 m.
- Each bike still leans its own amount, so the Prairie King 1200 is still the widest turner and the Cornhusker RR600 the tightest.

## 🏍️ Riding controls on screen
- While you're on a bike, the controls are listed in the bottom-right corner above the gauges, so you don't have to remember the clutch.
- The performance stats get out of the way while you ride.

## 🍺 Booze lasts 3x longer
- Each drink now lasts **90 seconds** instead of 30.
- At 8+ drinks you stay wasted for **6 minutes** before you start sobering up, up from 2.
- Chems are unchanged.

## ⚙️ Turn off the performance stats
- New setting: **Settings > Show performance stats (F3)**, which hides the readout in the bottom-right corner.
- Your choice is remembered. F3 still works.
- Settings now opens on top of the pause menu instead of underneath it.

---

## Install
**Windows:** unzip and run `BonksBunker.exe`.

**Mac:** unzip, then right-click `BonksBunker.app` > Open the first time (the app isn't notarized). If macOS says it's damaged, run `xattr -cr ` followed by the app's path in Terminal.

**Split downloads:** download every `.001`, `.002` ... part into one folder and open the `.001` with 7-Zip (Windows) or The Unarchiver (Mac). Or join them first. Windows: `copy /b file.zip.001+file.zip.002 file.zip`, Mac: `cat file.zip.0* > file.zip`.
